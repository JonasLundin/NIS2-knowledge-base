# Standards

Standards the directive, the implementing regulation and national schemes refer to (ISO/IEC 27001, IEC 62443, ETSI EN 319 401 and others), recorded as identifiers, lifecycle facts and links.

## Concepts

- [ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Information Security Management](iso-iec-27001.md) — International benchmark standard for information security management systems mapped to NIS2 Article 21 requirements.
- [ISA/IEC 62443](iec-62443.md) — Industrial automation and control systems cybersecurity standards applicable to operational technology in essential entities.
