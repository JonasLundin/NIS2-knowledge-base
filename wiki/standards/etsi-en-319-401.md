# Summary

ETSI EN 319 401 establishes general policy requirements for trust service providers, providing technical implementation baselines for electronic signatures and trust services under NIS2 and eIDAS.

# Normative Scope and OJEU Status
The standard specifies information security management, physical security, access control, and audit logging for trust services. Note: While widely recognized across EU supervisory schemes, individual ETSI standards must be formally cited in the Official Journal to confer presumption of conformity.
