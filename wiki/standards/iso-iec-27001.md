# Summary

**ISO/IEC 27001:2022** is the premier international standard for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an Information Security Management System (ISMS)[^directive-eu-2022-2555].

# Mapping to NIS2 Article 21
ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A controls provide a direct baseline satisfying the 10 mandatory technical measures under Article 21(2).
