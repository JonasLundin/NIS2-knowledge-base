# Summary

The Empowerments Register tracks delegated and implementing acts empowered under Directive (EU) 2022/2555[^directive-eu-2022-2555].

# Delegated and Implementing Powers
- **Article 21(5)**: Implementing acts laying down technical and methodological requirements of cybersecurity risk management measures (adopted: Regulation (EU) 2024/2690).
- **Article 23(11)**: Implementing acts specifying the cases in which an incident is considered to be significant (adopted: Regulation (EU) 2024/2690).
- **Article 24(2)**: Implementing acts mandating European cybersecurity certification schemes for specific categories of entities.
- **Article 27(6)**: Implementing acts laying down technical specifications and formats for the registration of digital entities.
