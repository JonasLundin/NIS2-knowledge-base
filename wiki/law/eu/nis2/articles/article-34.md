# Summary

**Article 34 (General conditions for imposing administrative fines on essential and important entities)** defines the financial enforcement powers of national competent authorities under Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2)[^directive-eu-2022-2555].

Article 34 establishes dissuasive, effective, and proportionate statutory ceilings modeled after the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

# Statutory Maximum Fines

### 1. Essential Entities (Article 34(4))
For breaches of Article 21 (risk-management measures) or Article 23 (incident reporting obligations), Member States must ensure competent authorities can impose maximum administrative fines of at least:
- **€10,000,000**, or
- **2% of the total worldwide annual turnover** in the preceding financial year of the undertaking to which the essential entity belongs,
whichever is higher.

### 2. Important Entities (Article 34(5))
For breaches of Article 21 or Article 23, competent authorities can impose maximum administrative fines of at least:
- **€7,000,000**, or
- **1.4% of the total worldwide annual turnover** in the preceding financial year of the undertaking to which the important entity belongs,
whichever is higher.

# Factors Influencing Fine Severity (Article 34(2))
Authorities must give due regard to:
1. Seriousness and duration of the infringement;
2. Previous infringements by the entity;
3. Damage caused to third parties and societal operations;
4. Intentional or negligent character of the infringement;
5. Measures taken by the entity to mitigate damage;
6. Level of cooperation with the competent authorities;
7. Manner in which the infringement became known (voluntary notification vs. whistleblowing).
