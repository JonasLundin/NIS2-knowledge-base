# Summary

Article 34 sets statutory ceilings and criteria for administrative fines imposed by Member States on essential and important entities for non-compliance with Article 21 (risk management) or Article 23 (incident reporting)[^directive-eu-2022-2555].

# Fine Thresholds
- **Essential Entities (Art 34(4))**: Member States must provide for maximum administrative fines of at least EUR 10 000 000 or 2 % of total worldwide annual turnover in the preceding financial year, whichever is higher.
- **Important Entities (Art 34(5))**: Member States must provide for maximum administrative fines of at least EUR 7 000 000 or 1.4 % of total worldwide annual turnover in the preceding financial year, whichever is higher.

# Statutory Criteria for Imposition
Supervisory authorities must consider the nature, gravity, duration of the infringement, intentional or negligent character, mitigation measures taken, degree of responsibility, previous infringements, and financial benefits gained.
