# Summary

**Article 21 (Cybersecurity risk-management measures)** is the substantive technical core of Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2)[^directive-eu-2022-2555].

Article 21 obligates essential and important entities to take appropriate and proportionate technical, operational, and organizational measures to manage the risks posed to the security of network and information systems, following an **all-hazards approach**.

# The 10 Mandatory Measures (Article 21(2))

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               ARTICLE 21(2) MANDATORY MEASURE MATRIX              |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| (a) Risk Analysis & Information System Security Policies          |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| (b) Incident Handling (Prevention, Detection, Response)           |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| (c) Business Continuity (Backup Management, Disaster Recovery)    |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| (d) Supply Chain Security (Supplier Due Diligence & Vulnerabilities)|
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| (e) Security in Network & Information Systems Acquisition & Dev   |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| (f) Effectiveness Assessment Policies (Audits, Penetration Tests) |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| (g) Basic Cyber Hygiene Practices & Cybersecurity Training        |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| (h) Cryptography & Encryption Policies and Procedures             |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| (i) Human Resources Security, Access Control & Asset Management   |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| (j) Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) & Secured Communications    |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

# Proportionate Application & Implementing Acts
- **Proportionality**: Measures must account for the entity's exposure to risks, size, likelihood and severity of incidents, and societal/economic impact.
- **Commission Implementing Regulation**: Pursuant to Article 21(5), the Commission adopts implementing acts specifying technical requirements for digital infrastructure and service providers (DNS, TLDs, cloud computing, data centers, CDNs, MSPs, online marketplaces, and search engines).
