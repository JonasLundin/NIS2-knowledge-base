# Articles

One page per article of Directive (EU) 2022/2555.

## Concepts

- [Article 1: Subject matter](article-1.md) — Subject matter, scope, and objectives of the NIS2 Directive.
- [Article 2: Scope & Size-Cap Rule](article-2.md) — Defines personal and material scope of NIS2 based on the size-cap rule (medium and large enterprises) and specific exceptions.
- [Article 3: Essential and Important Entities Classification](article-3.md) — Establishes statutory criteria distinguishing essential entities from important entities and supervisory implications.
- [Article 4: Sector-specific Union legal acts](article-4.md) — Relationship with lex specialis Union acts such as DORA and CER.
- [Article 5: Minimum harmonisation](article-5.md) — Right of Member States to adopt or maintain higher cybersecurity standards.
- [Article 6: Definitions](article-6.md) — Legal definitions of technical and regulatory terms used in the Directive.
- [Article 7: National cybersecurity strategy](article-7.md) — Obligation for each Member State to adopt a national cybersecurity strategy.
- [Article 8: Competent authorities and single points of contact](article-8.md) — Designation and powers of national competent authorities and SPOCs.
- [Article 9: Computer security incident response teams (CSIRTs)](article-9.md) — Designation, requirements, and operational capacities of national CSIRTs.
- [Article 10: Cooperation at national level](article-10.md) — Mechanisms for coordination among competent authorities, CSIRTs, and law enforcement.
- [Article 11: Coordinated vulnerability disclosure and a European vulnerability database](article-11.md) — Framework for national CVD policies and the ENISA European vulnerability database.
- [Article 12: Cooperation between CSIRTs](article-12.md) — Operational cross-border cooperation framework among designated CSIRTs.
- [Article 13: CSIRTs network](article-13.md) — Composition, tasks, and operational activities of the EU CSIRTs network.
- [Article 14: Cooperation Group](article-14.md) — Strategic cooperation body composed of Member States, Commission, and ENISA.
- [Article 15: European cyber crisis liaison organisation network (EU-CyCLONe)](article-15.md) — Crisis management network supporting coordinated management of large-scale cybersecurity incidents.
- [Article 16: Report on the state of cybersecurity in the Union](article-16.md) — Biennial ENISA report assessing cybersecurity posture and trends across the EU.
- [Article 17: Peer reviews](article-17.md) — Voluntary peer-review mechanism between Member States to enhance cybersecurity capabilities.
- [Article 18: Mutual assistance](article-18.md) — Cross-border supervisory assistance between national competent authorities.
- [Article 19: Joint supervisory activities](article-19.md) — Joint inspections and supervisory investigations by authorities across borders.
- [Article 20: Governance](article-20.md) — Mandates approval of cybersecurity measures by management bodies, personal liability, and mandatory training.
- [Article 21: Cybersecurity Risk-Management Measures](article-21.md) — Mandates ten baseline technical, operational, and organizational cybersecurity measures across an all-hazards approach.
- [Article 22: Union level coordinated risk assessments of critical supply chains](article-22.md) — Coordinated Union assessments of critical ICT supply chains.
- [Article 23: Reporting Obligations](article-23.md) — Establishes the mandatory multi-stage incident reporting framework with strict 24h, 72h, and 1-month statutory clocks.
- [Article 24: Use of European cybersecurity certification schemes](article-24.md) — Member State powers to mandate European cybersecurity certification for specific ICT products.
- [Article 25: Standardisation](article-25.md) — Encouraging use of international and European cybersecurity standards.
- [Article 26: Jurisdiction and territoriality](article-26.md) — Rules determining which Member State has jurisdiction over essential and important entities.
- [Article 27: Registry of entities](article-27.md) — Establishment of national registries and ENISA registry for cross-border entities.
- [Article 28: Database of domain name registration data](article-28.md) — Obligations for TLD registries and registrar entities regarding WHOIS data.
- [Article 29: Voluntary notification](article-29.md) — Procedures for voluntary reporting of incidents, threats, and near misses.
- [Article 30: Information sharing arrangements](article-30.md) — Framework enabling voluntary cybersecurity information exchange among entities.
- [Article 31: General aspects of supervision and enforcement](article-31.md) — Principles guiding supervisory action and enforcement proportionate to risk.
- [Article 32: Supervisory and enforcement measures in relation to essential entities](article-32.md) — Ex-ante and ex-post supervisory powers, audits, and binding instructions for essential entities.
- [Article 33: Supervisory and enforcement measures in relation to important entities](article-33.md) — Ex-post supervisory powers and evidence-based investigation for important entities.
- [Article 34: Administrative Fines and Penalties](article-34.md) — Establishes statutory administrative fine maximums up to €10M or 2% turnover for essential entities, and €7M or 1.4% turnover for important entities.
- [Article 35: Infringements entailing a personal data breach](article-35.md) — Coordination between cybersecurity authorities and data protection supervisory authorities under GDPR.
- [Article 36: Penalties](article-36.md) — Member State rules on penalties applicable to infringements of national transposing law.
- [Article 37: Exercise of the delegation](article-37.md) — Procedures for Commission delegated acts under the Directive.
- [Article 38: Committee procedure](article-38.md) — Assistance of the NIS Committee in adopting Commission implementing acts.
- [Article 39: Review](article-39.md) — Periodic European Commission review and evaluation of the Directive's application.
- [Article 40: Transposition](article-40.md) — Deadline for Member States to adopt and publish transposing laws by 17 October 2024.
- [Article 41: Transitional arrangements](article-41.md) — Arrangements for entities regulated under the original NIS Directive.
- [Article 42: Amendment of Regulation (EU) No 910/2014](article-42.md) — Consequential amendments to the eIDAS Regulation.
- [Article 43: Amendment of Directive (EU) 2018/1972](article-43.md) — Consequential amendments to the European Electronic Communications Code (EECC).
- [Article 44: Repeal](article-44.md) — Repeal of Directive (EU) 2016/1148 (NIS1) with effect from 18 October 2024.
- [Article 45: Entry into force](article-45.md) — Entry into force of the Directive on the twentieth day following publication in the OJEU.
- [Article 46: Addressees](article-46.md) — Directive is addressed to the Member States.
