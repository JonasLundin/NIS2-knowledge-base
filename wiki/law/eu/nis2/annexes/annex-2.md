# Summary

Annex II enumerates the seven **other critical sectors** governed by Directive (EU) 2022/2555[^directive-eu-2022-2555].

# Other Critical Sectors

1. **Postal and Courier Services**: Postal service providers and express delivery operators.
2. **Waste Management**: Undertakings carrying out waste management (excluding undertakings for which waste management is not their principal economic activity).
3. **Manufacture, Production and Distribution of Chemicals**: Undertakings manufacturing or distributing chemical substances and articles.
4. **Production, Processing and Distribution of Food**: Food businesses engaged in wholesale distribution, industrial production, or processing.
5. **Manufacturing**:
   - Manufacture of medical devices and in vitro diagnostic medical devices;
   - Manufacture of computer, electronic and optical products;
   - Manufacture of electrical equipment;
   - Manufacture of machinery and equipment n.e.c.;
   - Manufacture of motor vehicles, trailers and semi-trailers;
   - Manufacture of other transport equipment.
6. **Digital Providers**: Providers of online marketplaces, online search engines, and social networking services platforms.
7. **Research**: Research organisations.

# Penalties and Supervisory Status
Under Article 34(5), non-compliance by important entities is subject to administrative fines of a maximum of at least EUR 7 000 000 or 1.4 % of total worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher.
