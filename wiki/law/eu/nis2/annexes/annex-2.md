# Summary

**Annex II (Other Critical Sectors)** of Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2) enumerates the seven critical economic sectors brought under Union cybersecurity law for the first time[^directive-eu-2022-2555].

Medium and large enterprises operating within Annex II sectors are classified as **Important Entities**, subject to reactive (ex-post) supervision.

# Complete Taxonomy of the 7 Other Critical Sectors

| Sector | Subsectors & Qualifying Entity Types |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Postal & Courier Services** | Postal service providers, including express and parcel delivery services. |
| **2. Waste Management** | Undertakings engaged in the collection, transport, recovery, and disposal of waste (excluding waste water). |
| **3. Manufacture, Production & Distribution of Chemicals** | Undertakings manufacturing substances and distribution of chemical products. |
| **4. Production, Processing & Distribution of Food** | Food business operators engaged in wholesale distribution and industrial food production/processing. |
| **5. Manufacturing** | Manufacture of medical devices and in vitro diagnostic medical devices; Manufacture of computer, electronic, and optical products; Manufacture of electrical equipment; Manufacture of machinery and equipment n.e.c.; Manufacture of motor vehicles, trailers, and semi-trailers; Manufacture of other transport equipment. |
| **6. Digital Providers** | Providers of online marketplaces; Providers of online search engines; Providers of social networking services platforms. |
| **7. Research** | Research organisations (excluding educational institutions whose primary purpose is education). |

# Supervisory Distinctions for Annex II Entities
- Subject to ex-post supervision: authorities take enforcement action only when presented with evidence of non-compliance or following a reported cybersecurity incident.
- Maximum administrative fines capped at **€7,000,000** or **1.4% of total worldwide annual turnover**.
