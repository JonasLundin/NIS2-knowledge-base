# Summary

**Annex I: Sectors of High Criticality** of Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2)[^directive-eu-2022-2555].

Catalog of 11 sectors of high criticality whose qualifying operators are classified as essential entities.

# Sector Scope
Defines the economic and societal activities brought within the scope of Union cybersecurity obligations.
