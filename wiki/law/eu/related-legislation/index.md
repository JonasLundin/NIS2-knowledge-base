# Related Legislation

Navigation index for Related Legislation.

## Concepts

- [Directive (EU) 2022/2557 (Critical Entities Resilience)](cer-directive.md) — Directive on the resilience of critical entities (CER Directive), establishing physical and operational resilience requirements aligned with NIS2 Article 2(3).
- [Interplay between NIS2 and the Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)](cra-nis2-interplay.md) — Complementary legal architecture between product cybersecurity requirements (CRA) and operator resilience duties (NIS2).
- [Regulation (EU) 2019/881 (Cybersecurity Act)](cybersecurity-act.md) — Establishes ENISA's permanent mandate and the European cybersecurity certification framework referenced in NIS2 Article 24.
- [Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA)](dora-regulation.md) — Digital Operational Resilience Act for the financial sector, acting as lex specialis overriding NIS2 cybersecurity measures for financial entities.
- [Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 (eIDAS)](eidas-regulation.md) — Electronic identification and trust services regulation amended by NIS2 Article 43 to align trust service provider security.
- [Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR)](gdpr.md) — General Data Protection Regulation governing personal data processing, interfacing with NIS2 Article 35 incident notifications.
