# Summary

The **Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)** and **NIS2 Directive** form the dual pillars of European cybersecurity legislation[^directive-eu-2022-2555].

# Operational Interplay
- **CRA**: Applies to manufacturers of products with digital elements, ensuring secure-by-design hardware and software placed on the EU market.
- **NIS2**: Applies to operating entities, requiring continuous organizational risk management and incident reporting.
- **Presumption**: Procuring CRA-compliant products with CE marking assists NIS2 entities in satisfying supply chain due diligence under Article 21(2)(d).
