# Summary

The interplay between the Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847) and Directive (EU) 2022/2555 governs product security baselines and operational resilience duties[^regulation-eu-2024-2847][^directive-eu-2022-2555].

# Interplay Architecture
- **Product vs Entity Obligations**: CRA regulates manufacturers placing products with digital elements on the Union market, while NIS2 regulates operational cybersecurity management of essential and important entities.
- **Supply Chain Security**: Entities under NIS2 Article 21(2)(d) must account for vulnerabilities in their supply chain, leveraging CRA conformity assessments, technical documentation, and Software Bills of Materials (SBOMs).
- **Sister Knowledge Base**: For comprehensive details on CRA requirements, consult the [CRA Knowledge Base](https://github.com/JonasLundin/CRA-knowledge-base).
