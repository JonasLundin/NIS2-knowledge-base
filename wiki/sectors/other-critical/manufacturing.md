# Summary

**Manufacturing** sector under Annex II (Other Critical Sectors) of Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2)[^directive-eu-2022-2555].

Manufacture of medical devices, computer and electronic products, electrical equipment, machinery, motor vehicles, and other transport equipment.

# Entity Classification
Entities meeting size-cap thresholds in this sector are classified as **important entities** pursuant to Article 3, subject to ex-post supervisory mechanisms.
