# Annex I sectors of high criticality

One page per Annex I sector.

## Concepts

- [Sector: Banking](banking.md) — Credit institutions operating within the Union, subject to DORA lex specialis provisions.
- [Sector: Digital Infrastructure](digital-infrastructure.md) — IXPs, DNS service providers, TLD registries, cloud computing service providers, data centre service providers, CDNs, trust service providers, and public electronic communications networks.
- [Sector: Drinking Water](drinking-water.md) — Suppliers and distributors of water intended for human consumption.
- [Sector: Energy](energy.md) — Electricity, district heating and cooling, oil, natural gas, and hydrogen subsectors.
- [Sector: Financial Market Infrastructures](financial-market-infrastructures.md) — Operators of trading venues and central counterparties (CCPs).
- [Sector: Health](health.md) — Healthcare providers, EU reference laboratories, pharmaceutical research and manufacturing, and medical device manufacturers.
- [Sector: ICT Service Management (B2B)](ict-service-management.md) — Managed service providers (MSPs) and managed security service providers (MSSPs).
- [Sector: Public Administration](public-administration.md) — Public administration entities of central government and regional levels as designated by Member States.
- [Sector: Space](space.md) — Operators of ground-based infrastructure owned, managed, or operated by Member States or private parties supporting space services.
- [Sector: Transport](transport.md) — Air transport, rail transport, water transport, and road transport infrastructure and operators.
- [Sector: Waste Water](waste-water.md) — Undertakings collecting, treating, or disposing of urban, domestic, or industrial waste water.
