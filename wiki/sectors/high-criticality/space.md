# Summary

The **Space Sector** is designated as a sector of high criticality under Annex I of Directive (EU) 2022/2555[^directive-eu-2022-2555].

# Entity Types Included
- Operators of ground-based infrastructure owned, managed, or operated by Member States or private parties supporting space services

# Essential vs Important Classification
Under Article 3(1)(a) of NIS2:
- Entities operating in the Space Sector that **exceed the ceilings for medium-sized enterprises** (large enterprises with 250+ employees or turnover > EUR 50m and balance > EUR 43m) are classified as **essential entities**.
- Entities that qualify as **medium-sized enterprises** (50-249 employees, turnover <= EUR 50m, balance <= EUR 43m) are classified as **important entities** under Article 3(2), unless designated essential by a Member State under Article 2(2) or 2(3).
