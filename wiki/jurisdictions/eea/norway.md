# Summary

Norway (Norge) incorporates Directive (EU) 2022/2555 principles into the European Economic Area (EEA) agreement framework[^directive-eu-2022-2555].

# National Implementation Framework
- **Primary Statutory Act**: Lov om virksomheters digitale sikkerhet (Digitalsikkerhetsloven)
- **Competent Supervisory Authority**: Nasjonal sikkerhetsmyndighet (NSM)
- **National CSIRT**: NorCERT
