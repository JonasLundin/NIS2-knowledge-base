# Summary

Iceland (Ísland) incorporates Directive (EU) 2022/2555 principles into the European Economic Area (EEA) agreement framework[^directive-eu-2022-2555].

# National Implementation Framework
- **Primary Statutory Act**: Lög um öryggi net- og upplýsingakerfa mikilvægra innviða
- **Competent Supervisory Authority**: Fjarskiptastofa (Electronic Communications Office of Iceland)
- **National CSIRT**: CERT-IS
