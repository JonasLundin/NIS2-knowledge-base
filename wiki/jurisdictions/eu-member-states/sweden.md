# Summary

Sweden transposed Directive (EU) 2022/2555 through the **Cybersäkerhetslag (SFS 2025:1506)** and **Cybersäkerhetsförordning (SFS 2025:1507)**, entering into force on **15 January 2026**[^sfs-2025-1506].

# Competent Supervisory Authorities
Supervision is divided across sectoral lead authorities:
- **Energimyndigheten**: Energy sector (electricity, district heating, gas, oil).
- **Transportstyrelsen**: Transport sector (aviation, rail, maritime, road).
- **Finansinspektionen**: Banking and financial market infrastructures (coordinating with DORA).
- **Inspektionen för vård och omsorg (IVO)**: Healthcare delivery providers.
- **Läkemedelsverket**: Pharmaceuticals and medical devices.
- **Livsmedelsverket**: Drinking water and food production/distribution.
- **Post- och telestyrelsen (PTS)**: Electronic communications, DNS, TLDs, cloud, data centres, CDNs, MSPs, and digital providers.
- **Länsstyrelserna (six designated county boards)**: Regional/local public administration and waste management.

# National CSIRT and Coordination
- **Försvarets radioanstalt (FRA)**: Hosts **CERT-SE**, designated as the national CSIRT, national Single Point of Contact (SPOC), and cyber crisis coordinator under the law.
- **National Cybersecurity Centre (NCSC-SE)**: Inter-agency operational cooperation hub (FRA, Säkerhetspolisen, MSB, FM, PTS).

# Implementation Timelines
- **Notification Service Opening**: 2 February 2026 (entities must report their basic operational parameters as soon as possible).
- **Supervisory Enforcement**: Supervisors may issue requests following 14 days of opening.
- **Incident Reporting to CERT-SE**: Effective mandatory reporting begins 1 July 2026.
- **Risk Management & Management Training**: Statutory enforcement begins 1 October 2026.
*(Note: A proposed deadline of 2026-09-30 circulated in draft guidance was not codified in SFS 2025:1506/1507).*
