# Summary

Bulgaria (България) implements Directive (EU) 2022/2555 through national legislation and designated competent supervisory authorities[^directive-eu-2022-2555].

# National Transposition Framework
- **Primary Transposition Act / Bill**: Законопроект за изменение и допълнение на Закона за киберсигурност
- **Competent Supervisory Authority**: Ministry of Electronic Governance (Министерство на електронното управление)
- **National CSIRT**: CERT Bulgaria (CERT-BG)
- **Single Point of Contact (SPOC)**: Designated within national cybersecurity authorities.

# Supervisory Architecture and Penalties
Under Article 34 of NIS2, national statutory penalties must provide for fines of at least EUR 10 000 000 or 2% of turnover for essential entities, and at least EUR 7 000 000 or 1.4% for important entities.
