# Glossary

Navigation index for Glossary.

## Concepts

- [Cyber threat](cyber-threat.md) — A cyber threat as defined in Article 2(8) of Regulation (EU) 2019/881.
- [Cybersecurity](cybersecurity.md) — Cybersecurity as defined in Article 2(1) of Regulation (EU) 2019/881 (Cybersecurity Act).
- [Essential Entity](essential-entity.md) — An entity falling within Annex I and exceeding medium-sized enterprise thresholds, subject to ex-ante supervision.
- [Important Entity](important-entity.md) — An entity falling within Annex I or Annex II qualifying as a medium enterprise, subject to ex-post supervision.
- [Incident](incident.md) — An event compromising the availability, authenticity, integrity or confidentiality of stored, transmitted or processed data or services provided by network and information systems.
- [National cybersecurity strategy](national-cybersecurity-strategy.md) — A coherent framework of a Member State providing strategic objectives and priorities for the cybersecurity of network and information systems.
- [Near miss](near-miss.md) — An event that could have compromised the availability, authenticity, integrity or confidentiality of data or services, but was successfully thwarted or did not materialize.
- [Network and information system](network-and-information-system.md) — An electronic communications network, any device or group of interconnected devices executing automated processing of digital data, or digital data stored, processed, retrieved or transmitted.
- [Security of network and information systems](security-of-network-and-information-systems.md) — The ability of network and information systems to resist actions that compromise availability, authenticity, integrity, or confidentiality.
- [Significant incident](significant-incident.md) — An incident that has caused or is capable of causing severe operational disruption or substantial financial loss for the entity, or affected other persons by causing considerable damage.
- [Vulnerability](vulnerability.md) — A weakness, susceptibility or flaw of an ICT product or service that can be exploited by a cyber threat.
