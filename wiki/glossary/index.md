# Glossary

Terms defined in Article 6 of Directive (EU) 2022/2555.

## Concepts

- [Essential Entity](essential-entity.md) — An entity falling within Annex I and exceeding medium-sized enterprise thresholds, subject to ex-ante supervision.
- [Important Entity](important-entity.md) — An entity falling within Annex I or Annex II qualifying as a medium enterprise, subject to ex-post supervision.
- [Near Miss](near-miss.md) — An event that could have compromised availability, authenticity, integrity or confidentiality but was successfully thwarted.
- [Significant Incident](significant-incident.md) — An incident causing severe operational disruption or substantial material/non-material damage.
