# Summary

An **Important Entity** is a regulated entity subject to Directive (EU) 2022/2555 under Article 3(2), distinguished by ex-post supervisory mechanisms rather than ex-ante supervision[^directive-eu-2022-2555].

# Scope Coverage
1. Medium-sized entities operating in Annex I sectors of high criticality.
2. Medium-sized and large entities operating in Annex II other critical sectors.
3. Entities designated as important entities by Member States under national discretion.
