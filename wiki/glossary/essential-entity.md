# Definition

**Essential Entity**: An entity falling within Annex I and exceeding medium-sized enterprise thresholds, subject to ex-ante supervision.[^directive-eu-2022-2555]

# Context
Defined in Article 6 or Article 3 of Directive (EU) 2022/2555.
