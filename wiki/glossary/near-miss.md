# Summary

Article 6 of Directive (EU) 2022/2555 establishes the normative definition for **Near miss**[^directive-eu-2022-2555].

# Definition (Paraphrased)
An event that could have compromised the availability, authenticity, integrity or confidentiality of data or services, but was successfully thwarted or did not materialize.
