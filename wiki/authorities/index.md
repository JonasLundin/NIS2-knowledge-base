# Authorities

Navigation index for Authorities.

## Concepts

- [Competent Supervisory Authorities](competent-authorities.md) — National authorities designated under Article 8 of NIS2 responsible for cybersecurity supervision and regulatory enforcement.
- [CSIRTs Network](csirts-network.md) — Operational cooperation network established under Article 13 connecting national CSIRTs and CERT-EU.
- [Computer Security Incident Response Teams (CSIRTs)](csirts.md) — National operational units responsible for rapid incident response, analysis, and threat telemetry under Article 9.
- [European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA)](enisa.md) — Union agency providing technical expertise, maintaining registries, and supporting the CSIRTs network.
- [EU-CyCLONe](eu-cyclone.md) — European Cyber Crisis Liaison Organisation Network for large-scale incident crisis management under Article 15.
- [European Vulnerability Database (EUVD)](euvd.md) — Union-wide coordinated vulnerability database established under Article 12(2) and operated by ENISA.
- [NIS Cooperation Group](nis-cooperation-group.md) — Strategic cooperation body established under Article 14 to guide EU-wide cybersecurity policy.
- [Single Points of Contact (SPOC)](single-points-of-contact.md) — National liaison authorities designated under Article 8 to coordinate cross-border NIS2 cooperation.
