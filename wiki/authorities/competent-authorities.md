# Summary

National authorities designated under Article 8 of NIS2 responsible for cybersecurity supervision and regulatory enforcement.[^directive-eu-2022-2555]

# Responsibilities and Operational Duties
Under Directive (EU) 2022/2555, designated institutional actors maintain continuous operational capabilities, share cross-border threat telemetry, and coordinate supervisory actions across Union Member States.
