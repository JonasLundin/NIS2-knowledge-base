# Summary

**Article 23** of Directive (EU) 2022/2555 establishes strict, phased notification timelines for essential and important entities experiencing a **significant incident**[^directive-eu-2022-2555].

# The Phased Reporting Clocks

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               ARTICLE 23 STATUTORY REPORTING TIMELINE             |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. EARLY WARNING: within 24 HOURS of becoming aware               |
|    - Indicate whether caused by unlawful/malicious acts           |
|    - Indicate potential cross-border impact                       |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 2. INCIDENT NOTIFICATION: within 72 HOURS of becoming aware       |
|    - Initial assessment of severity, impact, and indicators       |
|      of compromise (IoCs)                                         |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 3. INTERMEDIATE REPORT: upon request of CSIRT/competent authority|
|    - Relevant status updates                                      |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 4. FINAL REPORT: no later than 1 MONTH after notification         |
|    - Detailed root-cause analysis, applied mitigations, and       |
|      definitive business impact                                   |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
```
