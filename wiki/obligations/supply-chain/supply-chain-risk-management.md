# Summary

**Article 21(2)(d)** and **Article 22** of Directive (EU) 2022/2555 mandate entities to assess and mitigate cybersecurity risks arising from supply chains and third-party supplier relationships[^directive-eu-2022-2555].

# Requirements for Entities (Article 21(3))
Entities must take into account:
1. Vulnerabilities specific to each direct supplier and service provider.
2. Overall quality and cybersecurity practices of suppliers, including secure development lifecycle.
3. Results of coordinated Union security risk assessments of critical supply chains conducted under Article 22.
