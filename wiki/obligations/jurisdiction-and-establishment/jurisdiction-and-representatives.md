# Summary

**Article 26** of Directive (EU) 2022/2555 sets out jurisdiction criteria and rules governing main establishments across the Union[^directive-eu-2022-2555].

# Key Jurisdiction Rules
1. **General Rule**: Entities are under the jurisdiction of the Member State in which they are established.
2. **One-Stop-Shop for Digital Infrastructure & ICT Providers**: DNS, TLD, cloud, CDN, and MSP providers are under the jurisdiction of the Member State where they have their **main establishment**.
3. **Non-EU Representative**: Entities not established in the Union offering services within the EU must designate a legal representative in one of the Member States where services are offered.
