# Summary

**Article 21** of Directive (EU) 2022/2555 mandates that essential and important entities take appropriate and proportionate technical, operational, and organizational measures to manage cybersecurity risks[^directive-eu-2022-2555].

# The 10 Mandatory Article 21(2) Measures
1. **Policies on Risk Analysis and Information System Security** (Point a)
2. **Incident Handling and Response** (Point b)
3. **Business Continuity, Backup Management, and Disaster Recovery** (Point c)
4. **Supply Chain Security and Supplier Due Diligence** (Point d)
5. **Security in Network and Information Systems Acquisition and Maintenance (CVD)** (Point e)
6. **Policies and Procedures to Assess Effectiveness of Risk Measures** (Point f)
7. **Basic Cyber Hygiene Practices and Cybersecurity Training** (Point g)
8. **Policies on Cryptography and Encryption** (Point h)
9. **Human Resources Security, Access Control, and Asset Management** (Point i)
10. **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and Secured Emergency Communications** (Point j)
