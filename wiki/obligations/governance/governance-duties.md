# Summary

**Article 20** of Directive (EU) 2022/2555 places direct accountability and governance obligations on the **management bodies** of essential and important entities[^directive-eu-2022-2555].

# Mandatory Governance Duties
1. **Approval of Risk Measures**: The management body must formally approve cybersecurity risk-management measures taken by the entity under Article 21.
2. **Oversight of Implementation**: Management bodies must oversee the continuous execution and effectiveness of measures and can be held liable for non-compliance.
3. **Mandatory Training**: Members of the management body must follow cybersecurity training to gain sufficient knowledge and skills to identify and assess risks.
4. **Employee Training**: Entities must offer similar regular cybersecurity training to their employees on an ongoing basis.

# Enforcement & Personal Liability (Article 32)
Competent authorities have powers to suspend certifications and, in the case of essential entities, temporarily prohibit individuals exercising managerial responsibilities from discharging managerial functions.
