# Summary

**Article 2** and **Article 3** establish the material scope of NIS2 using the **size-cap rule** based on Commission Recommendation 2003/361/EC[^directive-eu-2022-2555].

# Size-Cap Thresholds
- **Medium Enterprises**: $\ge 50$ employees or annual turnover / balance sheet total exceeding **€10 million**.
- **Large Enterprises**: $\ge 250$ employees or annual turnover exceeding **€50 million** (balance sheet total $> €43	ext{ million}$).
- **Exceptions (Entities In-Scope Regardless of Size)**:
  - Trust service providers, TLD registries, DNS service providers.
  - Providers of public electronic communications networks.
  - Sole national providers of an essential service.
